## Getting Started

### Creating an Account

1. Visit the portal homepage
2. Click **Sign Up**
3. Enter:
   - Email address
   - Password (min 8 characters, uppercase, lowercase, number)
   - Full name
   - Phone number (optional)
4. Verify email via link sent to your inbox
5. Log in with credentials

### Logging In

1. Click **Login**
2. Enter email and password
3. Complete 2FA if enabled
4. Access your dashboard

### Profile Management

After logging in, update your profile:
- **Name**: Your full name
- **Phone**: Contact number
- **Address**: Delivery address (for receipts)
- **Avatar**: Profile picture (optional)

## Online Seva Booking

### Available Sevas

| Seva | Description | Duration | Price |
|------|-------------|----------|-------|
| Suprabhatha Seva | Morning wake-up ritual | 30 min | ₹101 |
| Nijalu Seve | Flower offering | 15 min | ₹51 |
| Astothram | 108 names recitation | 45 min | ₹251 |
| Sahasranamarchana | 1008 names | 2 hours | ₹1001 |
| Abhishekam | Sacred bath | 1 hour | ₹501 |
| Tulasi Puja | Tulasi ceremony | 30 min | ₹151 |

### Booking Process

1. **Browse Sevas**
   - Go to **Sevas** page
   - Filter by category or price
   - Read descriptions

2. **Select Date & Time**
   - Choose booking date (up to 30 days in advance)
   - Select available time slot
   - Check slot availability

3. **Enter Details**
   - Your name
   - Gotra (optional)
   - Mobile number
   - Email
   - Special requests (optional)

4. **Make Payment**
   - Pay via UPI, Net Banking, or Card
   - Razorpay secure payment gateway
   - Get instant confirmation

5. **Receive Confirmation**
   - Email with booking details
   - SMS reminder
   - Booking ID for reference

### Managing Bookings

View all your bookings in **My Bookings**:

| Action | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| View Details | See full booking information |
| Download Receipt | Get PDF receipt |
| Cancel | Cancel booking (24h notice) |
| Reschedule | Change date/time (if available) |

### Cancellation Policy

- Cancel up to **24 hours** before scheduled time
- **10% cancellation fee** applied
- Refund processed within 5-7 business days
- No cancellation within 24 hours

## AI Assistant (Raya)

### About Raya

Raya is your AI-powered temple assistant, available 24/7 to answer questions about:
- Temple timings
- Sevas and booking
- Festivals
- Rituals and traditions
- General inquiries

### How to Use

1. **Find the Chat**
   - Look for chat icon (bottom-right)
   - Click to open chat window

2. **Ask Questions**
   - Type your question in Kannada or English
   - Examples:
     - "What is the temple timing?"
     - "How to book Astothram?"
     - "When is Raghavendra Aradhana?"

3. **Get Instant Response**
   - AI provides immediate answers
   - Sources temple knowledge base
   - Can switch to human support if needed

### Language Support

Raya understands:
- English
- Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ)
- Mixed language queries

### Feedback

Help improve Raya:
- Click 👍 or 👎 on responses

## FAQs

### General Questions

**Q: What are temple timings?**
A: Morning 5:00 AM - 1:00 PM, Evening 4:00 PM - 8:30 PM

**Q: Is there a dress code?**
A: Traditional clothing recommended. Decorated clothing not permitted inside sanctum.

**Q: Can I visit without booking?**
A: Yes, darshan is open during temple hours. Sevas require booking.

### Booking Questions

**Q: How far in advance can I book sevas?**
A: Up to 30 days in advance.

**Q: Can I book for family members?**
A: Yes, mention names in special requests.

**Q: What if I need to cancel?**
A: Cancel 24 hours before for refund (minus 10% fee).

### Donation Questions

**Q: Is my donation secure?**
A: Yes, payments processed via Razorpay (PCI compliant).

**Q: How do I get tax receipt?**
A: Auto-generated for ₹500+. Download from donor portal.

**Q: Can I donate for specific purpose?**
A: Yes, select campaign or mention in notes.

### Technical Questions

**Q: Why didn't I receive confirmation email?**
A: Check spam folder. Verify email registered correctly.

**Q: Payment failed but amount deducted?**
A: Contact support with transaction ID. Amount refunded in 5-7 days.
